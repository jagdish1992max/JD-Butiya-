About me

Designer by eye,
developer by logic.

Main Jaipur ki pink-city galiyon se design ka kaam shuru kiya — aur pehli website apne phone par banayi thi. Ek chhoti si screen, badi si zid. Aaj wahi zid brands ke liye premium, fast websites mein badalti hai.

Mera formula simple hai: pehle brand ko samjho, phir user ko, phir pixels ko. Templates nahi, har project custom — kyunki aapka business kisi aur jaisa nahi hai.

Jab main code nahi likh raha, toh probably chai pe raha hoon, ya cricket khel raha hoon, ya naye fonts dekh raha hoon. Teeno mein detail matter karti hai.
